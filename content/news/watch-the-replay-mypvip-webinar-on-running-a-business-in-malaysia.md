## The recording is up

MYPVIP's free webinar on running a business in Malaysia took place on Sunday 1 November, and the full recording is now available above. If you couldn't make it live, or want to go back over the details, you can watch it at your own pace.

PVIP consultant Chee Mee Ho hosted the session, with guest speaker Datin Jiniy Seelan, managing partner of JS Partners Chartered Accountant.

## What it covers

The session starts with why Malaysia works as a base for doing business across Southeast Asia, then explains what PVIP lets you do. The pass gives up to 20 years of residency, has no minimum stay, and lets you work and run your own company.

It then turns to the company itself: why most founders choose a Sdn Bhd over a branch or representative office, which sectors allow 100% foreign ownership, and how incorporation runs through SSM. Datin Jiniy Seelan shares a case where a founder's business activity codes didn't match the actual business, and fixing it took more than three months.

The last part covers what comes after incorporation, including tax registration, the corporate bank account, hiring, and why the tighter Employment Pass rules from June 2026 don't apply to PVIP founders.
