## Guidance aims to streamline applications

Forest City announced a package of guidance notes that outline the documentation and procedural requirements for the Special Economic Zone (SFZ) version of the Malaysia My Second Home (MM2H) programme. The notes are meant to provide a clearer pathway for prospective long‑term residents, reducing uncertainty around the submission process and helping applicants understand the steps involved before they lodge a formal request.

While the guidance does not alter the core MM2H criteria set by the Ministry of Tourism, Arts and Culture, it does specify how the SFZ framework will be applied locally. The document highlights the need for applicants to coordinate with the designated Forest City authority and to ensure that all supporting paperwork meets the outlined standards.

## Regional demand drives focus

Interest in Malaysia’s MM2H scheme has risen sharply among citizens of neighbouring countries, prompting developers such as Forest City to tailor information for this audience. The guidance acknowledges this trend and seeks to position the SFZ as an attractive entry point for those looking for a long‑term residency option that is perceived as straightforward compared with other programmes in the region.

By publishing the guidance, Forest City hopes to capture a share of the growing market and to demonstrate that the SFZ can accommodate the lifestyle and investment expectations of regional applicants. The emphasis on clarity is intended to reassure potential residents that the process is transparent and manageable.

## Next steps for prospective residents

Potential applicants are advised to review the newly released guidance closely and to contact the Forest City liaison office for any clarifications. The notes do not constitute a legal or tax advisory document, so individuals should seek professional advice where necessary before committing to any financial or residency decisions.

Until the Forest City authority finalises the operational details, interested parties should monitor official channels for updates. Compliance with the outlined requirements will be essential for a successful application, and early preparation can help avoid delays once the formal submission window opens.
