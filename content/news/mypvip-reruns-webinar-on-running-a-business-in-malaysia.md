## A second chance to join live

MYPVIP is running its 1 November webinar on running a business in Malaysia a second time. If you missed it, or watched the recording and now have questions of your own, this is the time to ask them.

[MYPVIP](https://mypvip.com/) is hosting "Unlocking Malaysia's Business Opportunities: A Practical Guide for PVIP Applicants" on Zoom on Wednesday 18 November at 8pm Malaysia time (UTC+8). It is free to join.

## Who you'll hear from

The speakers are PVIP consultant Chee Mee Ho and guest speaker Datin Jiniy Seelan, managing partner of JS Partners Chartered Accountant. Both are MYPVIP business associates.

## What it covers

The session runs in three parts. First, PVIP as more than a residency: what the pass lets you do, and why it can be your gateway to Southeast Asia. Second, setting up your company: how incorporation works in Malaysia. Third, launching and growing your business: a roadmap from PVIP to operations.

## Who should join

Business owners and professionals thinking of moving to Malaysia and setting up a company here. Families looking at a long-stay visa for Malaysia. And anyone who has already incorporated a Malaysian company and is now working out the visa side.

## Can't make the date?

The 1 November session is recorded in full. You can [watch the replay](/news/watch-the-replay-mypvip-webinar-on-running-a-business-in-malaysia/), and our [written guide to PVIP and company set-up](/insights/how-to/pvip-and-company-incorporation-malaysia/) sets out the figures from it stage by stage.

## Save your place

Registration is free and takes a minute on Zoom. Register now, and bring your questions.
