## 7,650 applications in under two years

Malaysia's long-term residency programme continues to attract international applicants, with new figures highlighting demand from China and Taiwan and plans to expand promotion in other Asian markets.

According to a statement by Deputy Tourism, Arts and Culture Minister Chiew Choon Man, reported by Bernama on 26 September 2026, the Malaysia My Second Home (MM2H) programme received 7,650 new applications between 1 October 2024 and 31 August 2026.

China accounted for 3,847 applications, followed by Taiwan with 1,054. Together, they represented approximately 64% of the total. Hong Kong contributed 324 applications, Singapore 287 and the United States 284.

## RM8.85 billion in economic value

The minister also reported RM8.85 billion in economic value associated with the programme, including more than RM5 billion in property purchases and RM3.82 billion in fixed deposits. These figures cover applications across the Platinum, Gold, Silver and Special Economic Zone categories.

The application total should be read carefully: it represents submissions over the reporting period, rather than the number of approved participants. It also does not, by itself, establish whether demand has accelerated in recent months.

## Sales missions to Hong Kong, Japan and South Korea

Malaysia is preparing to broaden the programme's international reach. The MM2H One Stop Centre plans overseas sales missions targeting Hong Kong, Japan and South Korea. The announcement did not specify dates or participation arrangements.

## What it means if you are considering Malaysia

For readers considering Malaysia as a long-term home, these developments provide useful market context. Choosing a suitable residency route still requires a personal assessment of family needs, available funds, preferred location and intended activities in Malaysia.

Before making financial commitments, prospective applicants should obtain a clear explanation of the relevant programme conditions, total costs and application process. A carefully planned application begins with choosing a route that fits how you and your family intend to live.
