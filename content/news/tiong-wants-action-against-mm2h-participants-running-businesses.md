## Enforcement is coming for the business side

Plenty of people move to Malaysia on MM2H and then, a year or two in, start something on the side. A café, a consultancy, a few rental units run as a business. Most never checked whether their pass allowed it. Tourism, Arts and Culture Minister Tiong King Sing now wants that checked for them.

According to Free Malaysia Today, Tiong has instructed immigration and the related agencies to tighten oversight of MM2H holders and what they do once they're here. "Appropriate action must be taken to ensure compliance with MM2H conditions and prevent abuse of the programme," he said.

His main concern is participants running businesses when their category doesn't permit it.

## Who is allowed to work

Under the current rules, only Platinum holders may work, invest or operate a business. Gold, Silver and the special economic and financial zone (SEZ/SFZ) categories need further authorisation before they take on any commercial activity.

That lines up with the MM2H guide, which bars business and investment activity and career opportunities on the Silver and Gold tiers. If you're on one of those tiers and already trading, you're the person this announcement is about.

## Vetting delays were on the table too

At the same meeting Tiong raised two complaints applicants already know well: security vetting that drags, and processing times that vary from one file to the next. Every MM2H application goes through police background screening, and each applicant aged 18 or over has to supply a certificate of good conduct.

He didn't say what would change, or when.

## The programme as it stands

MM2H started in 2002, was suspended in August 2020, came back with revised conditions, and was restructured again in 2024. For each tier's pass length, fixed deposit and property rules, see the MM2H guide linked at the end of this article.

If you picked Silver or Gold for the lower deposit and planned to earn locally, the gap between what you want to do and what your pass allows is now something the government has said it will look for. Sort out the authorisation, or rethink the tier, before an officer raises it with you.
