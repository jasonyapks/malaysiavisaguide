## Eligibility for Salaried Professionals

The new UAE Golden Visa grants a decade of residence without requiring a local sponsor. Indian professionals become eligible when their monthly salary reaches AED 30,000, a level commonly met by senior managers and technical specialists. Applicants must also hold a recognised bachelor's degree and a valid employment contract with a UAE‑based employer. This pathway is designed to reduce the administrative burden of frequent visa renewals.

By uncoupling residency from a single employer, the scheme offers greater flexibility for career moves within the Gulf. Holders can change jobs or negotiate better terms without fearing immediate loss of status. The long‑term nature of the permit also simplifies travel planning and provides a stable base for families living abroad.

## Investment Route for Property Buyers

Indian investors can secure the ten‑year visa by committing at least AED 2 million to UAE real‑estate. The investment may be made in off‑plan projects or in properties that are mortgaged, allowing a range of financing options. This threshold targets buyers of luxury homes and high‑value developments, reinforcing the country's aim to attract capital alongside talent.

The property route not only guarantees residency for the investor but also extends the same right to their immediate family. Ownership of a qualifying asset provides a tangible anchor for capital while delivering a secure environment for spouses and children, reinforcing the UAE’s position as a global hub for wealth preservation.

## Family Sponsorship Benefits

A key advantage of the Golden Visa is the ability to sponsor spouses and children of any age, with no cap on the number of domestic workers that can be included. Should the primary visa holder pass away, the family members remain covered until the ten‑year period expires, ensuring continuity of residence.

These provisions create a sense of permanence for Indian families considering a long‑term move to the Gulf. The inclusive sponsorship rules allow households to plan education, employment and lifestyle decisions with confidence, knowing that their residency status is insulated from individual employment changes.
