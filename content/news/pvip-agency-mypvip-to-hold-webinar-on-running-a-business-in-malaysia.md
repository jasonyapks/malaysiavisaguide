## You're invited

If you're thinking about moving to Malaysia and starting a company here, you've probably found that the visa and the business get explained separately. On Sunday 1 November, one free session brings the two together, and you're welcome to join.

[MYPVIP](https://mypvip.com/) is hosting "From PVIP Visa to Business Owner: Unlocking Malaysia's Business Opportunities" on Zoom at 8pm Malaysia time (UTC+8). It's a practical guide for PVIP applicants, in English, free to join, and it ends with a live Q&A.

## Who you'll hear from

Your host is PVIP consultant Chee Mee Ho, with guest speaker Datin Jiniy Seelan, managing partner of JS Partners Chartered Accountant. Both are MYPVIP business associates.

## What you'll learn

The session runs in three parts. First, PVIP as more than a residency: what the pass lets you do, and why it can be your gateway to Southeast Asia. Second, setting up your company: how incorporation works in Malaysia. Third, launching and growing your business: a roadmap from PVIP to operations.

## Who should join

Business owners and professionals thinking of moving to Malaysia and setting up a company here. Families looking at a long-stay visa for Malaysia. And anyone who has already incorporated a Malaysian company and is now working out the visa side.

## Why PVIP and a business fit together

PVIP holders may live, work and run a business in Malaysia on the pass itself, without a separate work permit and without their company sponsoring them. That is the main reason founders look at PVIP rather than MM2H, whose Silver and Gold tiers do not allow work.

PVIP is a significant commitment, so it's worth hearing how it works before you decide. The PVIP guide linked at the end of this article sets out the fees, the income requirement and the timeline.
