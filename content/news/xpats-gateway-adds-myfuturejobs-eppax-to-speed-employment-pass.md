## Integrated digital platform

The Ministry of Human Resources announced that the Xpats Gateway platform now hosts the MYFutureJobs and ePPAx modules, creating an end‑to‑end digital ecosystem for expatriate Employment Pass applications. The upgrade, which became operational on 1 July 2025, links 26 agencies, including PERKESO, JTKSM and TalentCorp, under a single‑window interface, reducing the need for separate submissions.

Minister of Human Resources YB Steven Sim Chee Keong highlighted that the move demonstrates Malaysia’s drive to improve regulatory coherence and boost productivity. He noted that the streamlined approach supports the broader MADANI Economy agenda by making the country more attractive to foreign talent and investment.

## Simplified employer workflow

Employers hiring expatriates can now fulfil mandatory Employment Act requirements, obtain MYFutureJobs acknowledgment letters and submit ePPAx documentation without leaving the Xpats Gateway portal. The integration eliminates repetitive data entry, with early estimates suggesting a reduction of up to 70% for companies registered with the Economic Service Department (ESD).

The new system also shortens approval timelines: both JTKSM and MYFutureJobs clearances are processed within 10 working days each. Applications can be lodged concurrently, meaning that the overall time from submission to Employment Pass issuance is significantly faster for all sectors.

## Clearer timelines and security

A revised Client Charter now provides explicit service standards, and employers can monitor the status of their applications in real time. Because all documents are pulled directly from official sources such as the Companies Commission of Malaysia, PERKESO and JTKSM, the risk of fraudulent submissions is markedly reduced.

The streamlined approval process has already contributed to Malaysia’s rise of 11 places in the 2025 IMD World Competitiveness Ranking, reflecting gains in business efficiency and digital readiness. Officials see Xpats Gateway as a cornerstone of the nation’s ambition to rank among the world’s top 12 competitive economies by 2033.
