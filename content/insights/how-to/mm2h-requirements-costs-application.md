You've read five pages about MM2H, and they don't agree. One says you need RM40,000 a month in offshore income. Another says the deposit is RM1 million. A third says you can be in from RM600,000. An agent's brochure leaves out the property purchase until page four.

Here's the thing most of those pages won't tell you. A lot of them describe a programme that no longer exists. MM2H was rewritten in 2021, and again in 2024, and the internet never caught up with either. So you're not bad at research. You're reading three different sets of rules at once.

This page sets out the current one, as published when we last checked it. If a figure changes before or during 2027, it changes here.

**The short answer:** MM2H now has three main tiers, Silver, Gold and Platinum. Each asks for a fixed deposit, a compulsory property purchase and a one-off set of fees. You must be at least {{mm2h-silver:minAge:number}}. There's no published income test. Silver is the cheapest way in. Platinum is the only tier that lets you work.

Every pass is renewable. Beyond the table, four things apply on every tier.

- **No income test, but not "no questions".** {{mm2h-silver:incomePractice.note:text}} ((MYPVIP practice, as at 28 July 2026))
- **A clean record and a clean bill of health.** You need a police clearance from your country of residence, a medical examination at an approved clinic in Malaysia, and medical insurance valid in Malaysia.
- **The stay rule depends on your age, not your tier.** {{mm2h-silver:minStayPerYear:text}}
- **Your family can come with you.** A spouse, parents, parents-in-law and unmarried children up to age 34 can be included as dependants.

There are also two zone routes, SEZ and SFZ, tied to designated areas of Johor. They're cheaper and narrower, and they work differently. They have [their own guide](/insights/comparisons/mm2h-sez-sfz-forest-city-2026/).

## What MM2H actually costs

Most MM2H quotes are hard to compare because they mix three kinds of money. Separate them and the picture gets simple.

**Fees you pay and don't get back.** For one main applicant, the agency fee plus the participation fee comes to about **RM41,000 on Silver, RM58,000 on Gold and RM270,000 on Platinum**. The agency fee is fixed by the government, not by the agency: {{mm2h-silver:agencyFee.principal:money}}, {{mm2h-gold:agencyFee.principal:money}} and {{mm2h-platinum:agencyFee.principal:money}}. {{mm2h-silver:agencyFee.paymentTerms:text}} ((MYPVIP, from the government MM2H fee schedule, as at 28 July 2026))

**Each dependant adds** a processing fee of {{mm2h-silver:processingFee.dependant:money}}, a pass fee of {{mm2h-silver:passFeePerYear.dependant:money}} a year for the first five years, and a visa fee set by their nationality. The agency fee covers your first dependant. From the second, it adds {{mm2h-silver:agencyFee.perDependant:money}} each.

**Capital you park and keep.** The fixed deposit sits in a Malaysian bank in your name. {{mm2h-silver:fixedDepositWithdrawable:text}} The rest stays in the account while you hold the pass.

**Capital you spend on a home you own.** The property is compulsory. The figure in the table is only the programme's floor, and the state's floor for foreign buyers is often higher: RM1,000,000 in Kuala Lumpur and RM2,000,000 in Selangor. [Our MM2H property guide](/insights/how-to/mm2h-property-rules/) covers the rules and the costs on top of the price.

For every line item, including the ones most quotes leave out, see [how much MM2H costs](/insights/how-to/how-much-does-mm2h-cost/).

## How the application runs

The order matters more than the paperwork. Some steps can only happen in Malaysia, and one can't start until you're already approved.

1. **Build your file at home.** Passport copies, police clearance, evidence of your financial position and a medical declaration. Start the police clearance first. In most countries, it's the slowest document.
2. **File through a licensed agent and wait for the Conditional Approval Letter.** Nothing else can begin until it's issued, so file cleanly rather than quickly.
3. **Pay and come to Malaysia within 90 days of approval.** You pay the rest of the agency fee and the government fees, open a bank account, place the deposit, do the medical and buy your insurance.
4. **Have the pass endorsed in Putrajaya.** The multi-year visa goes into your passport. Your one-year window to complete the property purchase starts here.

The full sequence, with what goes wrong at each stage, is in [how to apply for MM2H](/insights/how-to/apply-for-mm2h-2026/).

**Check your agent's licence before you pay anything.** Ask for their MOTAC licence number and look it up yourself on [MOTAC's list of licensed MM2H companies](https://www.motac.gov.my/en/kategori-semakan-new/licensed-mm2h-company/). An agency that isn't on the list isn't a cheaper option. It's an unlicensed one.

## Which tier is yours?

The tiers don't give you different lives in Malaysia. Silver and Gold holders live in the same condos, use the same hospitals and pay the same school fees. What changes is how long you're covered, how much capital you tie up, and whether you can work.

**You want the lowest commitment and you're still deciding how long you'll stay.** Silver. The deposit is the smallest, and renewing every five years is a reasonable price for keeping your options open. Watch the property floor: if you're buying in Kuala Lumpur or Selangor, the state's floor binds, not Silver's.

**You know you're staying, and the money would sit in a bank anyway.** Gold. You buy fifteen years of certainty and skip two renewals. If the home you want costs over RM1,000,000 regardless, Gold's property rule costs you nothing extra. [Silver vs Gold vs Platinum](/insights/comparisons/mm2h-silver-vs-gold-vs-platinum/) runs the numbers.

**You need to work or run a business in Malaysia.** Platinum, or a different programme. Platinum is the only tier with work rights, and its fees are far higher. If work is the whole point, read [MM2H Platinum vs PVIP](/insights/comparisons/mm2h-platinum-vs-pvip/) before you commit.

**You don't want to buy property at all.** Then federal MM2H isn't for you, on any tier. Look at [Sarawak's version of MM2H](/insights/comparisons/federal-mm2h-vs-sarawak-mm2h/), which asks for no purchase, or at PVIP.

## What could change in 2027

At the time of review, no changes to MM2H for 2027 had been announced. But the programme has been rewritten twice in five years, so expect change to be possible. Two things are worth doing.

First, check the review date at the top of any page you rely on, including this one. Second, ask your agent in writing which version of the guidelines your application will be assessed under. If the rules change mid-application, you want to know which side of the line you're on.
