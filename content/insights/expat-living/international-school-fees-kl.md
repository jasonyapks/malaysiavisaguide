If you're moving to Malaysia with children, you've probably started with the visa and the house. Most families do, and most of them find out later that they started in the wrong place. In Kuala Lumpur, two children at one of the established international schools can cost more than your rent, your car and your groceries put together.

There's a second surprise too. Your child's right to sit in that classroom depends on your pass. Some passes need a separate permission before term starts, and every pass has an age when your child stops counting as your dependant.

**The short answer:** for 2026/27, the established international schools in Kuala Lumpur charge roughly RM85,000 to RM145,000 a year, and a foreign family pays 6% service tax on top of most of them. In Penang, a child starting primary costs about RM31,800 a year, little more than a third of the Kuala Lumpur price. If you're on MM2H, apply for your child's permission to study as soon as you have the school's offer.

## What six schools charge for 2026/27

We've compared six schools that publish their 2026/27 fees in full, four in or near Kuala Lumpur and two in Penang. They're well established and widely used by foreign families. It isn't a ranking, and a school that isn't here isn't a worse choice.

| School | Where | Curriculum | Year 1 / Grade 1 | Year 7 / Grade 7 | Final years |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **ISKL** | Ampang Hilir, KL | American and IB | 115,600 | 130,600 | 143,400 |
| **Garden International School** | Mont Kiara, KL | British | 85,590 | 118,170 | 126,870 |
| **The Alice Smith School** | Kuala Lumpur | British | 89,070 | 112,950 | 122,370 |
| **Nexus International School** | Putrajaya | British and IB | 59,460 | 86,040 | 108,420 |
| **Uplands** | Penang | British and IB | 31,800 | 71,400 | 74,400 |
| **Dalat International School** | Penang | American | 31,800 | 57,200 | 59,000 |
[^1]: RM a year, tuition only, each school's published 2026/27 schedule, read 23 October 2026. Termly or semester fees have been multiplied out. Before 6% service tax, which applies to annual fees above RM60,000. Uniforms, buses, meals, trips and devices are extra at every school.

The gap between the cities is the first thing to see. A child starting Year 1 costs about RM31,800 a year in Penang and RM85,000 to RM116,000 in Kuala Lumpur. If your work doesn't tie you to the capital, that difference alone can decide where you live.

## The 6% that local families don't pay

Since 1 July 2025, private and international schools charging more than **RM60,000 per student per academic year** collect 6% service tax on their fees. The Customs Department's exemption covers Malaysian citizens and holders of a Malaysian disability (OKU) card. A foreign family pays it.

On the numbers above, that's an extra **RM5,000 to RM9,000 a year per child** at the Kuala Lumpur schools. ISKL's top fee of RM143,400 becomes about RM152,000. The tax also applies to application and registration fees for new students, which is why the next section matters.

## What you pay before the first day

Tuition isn't the whole bill. Every school charges something when you join, and some of it is large:

- **ISKL:** RM1,800 to apply, then a **one-off RM59,000 registration fee** for a new student from Prep Senior to Grade 12. It's non-refundable, and 6% SST applies to it.
- **The Alice Smith School:** RM1,500 to apply, a RM20,000 enrolment fee, and a refundable parent deposit of RM24,000 from Year 1.
- **Nexus:** RM1,300 to apply, a registration fee of RM10,000 in Early Years plus another RM10,000 on entering Year 1, and a refundable deposit equal to one term's tuition.
- **Uplands:** about RM800 to apply, RM1,000 association membership, a RM7,000 entrance fee and a refundable RM5,000 deposit.
- **Dalat:** RM800 to apply, RM300 for the placement test and a refundable RM1,500 deposit.
- **Garden International School:** RM1,590 to apply, including service tax, a RM20,000 registration fee from Year 1, and a refundable deposit that is topped up as your child moves up the school.

For a family with two children moving to Kuala Lumpur, the non-refundable joining fees alone can come to RM40,000 to RM130,000 before the first term's tuition. Deposits come on top. Those come back when you leave, as long as you give the notice the school asks for. The rest doesn't.

## Your child's pass: what each visa allows

Every pass that lets you bring your children lets them go to school. The differences are in the paperwork and in how long they stay covered:

| Your pass | Children covered until | Before they start school |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **MM2H** | Age 34, if unmarried | Apply to MOTAC for Permission to Study (14 working days) |
| **PVIP** | Under 25 | Ask the school what it needs to see |
| **Sarawak MM2H** | 21 and under | Ask the school what it needs to see |
| **Employment Pass** | Under 18 | Ask the school what it needs to see |
[^2]: Age limits are each programme's dependant rules as published on this site. The MM2H permission is item 6 of MOTAC's MM2H Application Guidelines Booklet, May 2026, which asks for the school's offer letter and proof that the enrolment fee has been paid.

Two things in that table catch families out.

**On MM2H, the school comes first, then the permission.** MOTAC wants the school's offer letter and proof you've paid the enrolment fee before it processes the application. So you're committing money to a school before your child's permission is approved. Allow at least three weeks between paying and the first day of term.

**On an Employment Pass, your children age out at 18.** That's often the year they're taking final exams. A child who turns 18 mid-course needs a pass of their own, usually a Student Pass, and the switch needs planning. MM2H covers children for much longer, which is one reason families with teenagers look at it.

## Which school, and which city, is yours?

**Your employer pays the fees.** Then choose the school first and live near it. In Kuala Lumpur that usually means Mont Kiara for Garden International School, or Ampang Hilir for ISKL. Your commute is the employer's problem, and your children's isn't. Read [where foreigners live in KL](/insights/expat-living/kl-neighbourhoods-rent-for-expats/) for what rent costs near each school.

**You're paying yourself, and your work isn't tied to Kuala Lumpur.** Look hard at Penang. A child in Year 7 costs RM71,400 a year at Uplands against RM118,170 at Garden International School. Over five years of secondary school, the difference is more than RM230,000. [KL vs Penang vs Johor Bahru](/insights/comparisons/kl-vs-penang-vs-johor-bahru-cost-of-living/) compares the rest of the budget.

**You want the IB Diploma.** ISKL, Nexus and Uplands offer it among these six. Nexus's final-year fee is about RM35,000 a year less than ISKL's.

**You want an American curriculum.** ISKL in Kuala Lumpur or Dalat in Penang. Dalat's Grade 9 to 12 fee of RM59,000 is under the RM60,000 service-tax line. ISKL's is RM143,400 before tax.

**You have teenagers and you're choosing a visa.** Check the age limit before you choose. An Employment Pass stops covering them at 18. MM2H keeps them as dependants until 34, and PVIP until 25.

((Fees are each school's published 2026/27 schedule, read 23 October 2026. Schools review fees every year. Service tax applies to annual fees above RM60,000 and to some one-off fees. Verify the current figures and your child's pass requirements with the school before you commit.))
