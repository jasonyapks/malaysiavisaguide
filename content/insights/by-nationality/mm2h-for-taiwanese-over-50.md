The objection we hear most from Taiwanese enquirers is not the deposit. It is time. You have a business that needs you, parents in Taichung, grandchildren in Taipei, a doctor you trust, a household registration you do not want to lose. Spending three months of every year in Malaysia is not on the table, so a long-stay visa that demands it is not one you can hold.

Under 50, MM2H does demand it. From 50, it does not. That single rule is what makes MM2H workable for most Taiwanese families we speak to, and it is worth understanding exactly what it gives you and what it leaves in place.

## The 90-day rule, and why it falls away at 50

MOTAC's guide sets the minimum stay by age rather than by tier (p.19). In full: {{mm2h-silver:minStayPerYear:text}}

For an applicant aged 49, that is roughly one quarter in Malaysia every year, for every year of the pass. For an applicant aged 50 or over, there is no number to meet. You can come for four weeks over Chinese New Year and three weeks in autumn, or for one long winter, or skip a year when a parent is unwell. Nothing in the pass counts.

The age floor for the pass itself is {{mm2h-silver:minAge:number}} on Silver, Gold and Platinum, with no upper limit (p.17). An applicant in their seventies applies on the same terms as one in their fifties.

## Why the visa-free 30 days is not the answer

A Taiwan passport already gets you into Malaysia without a visa. The Bureau of Consular Affairs records that Malaysia gives ROC nationals 30 days visa-free per entry, and its own page warns that frequent visa-free entries can draw questions from immigration officers.

That is a visitor's status. It works for a holiday. It does not give you a pass that lasts years, a status to buy property under, a way to register a spouse or parents as dependants, or any certainty that the next entry will be as easy as the last. MM2H does those things, and from 50 it does them without asking for more of your time than a visitor would spend.

## Your 戶籍 and 健保: what MM2H does not change

Two Taiwan rules matter more to a part-year life than anything in MOTAC's guide. Neither is affected by holding MM2H; both are affected by how long you are away.

**Household registration (戶籍).** Article 16 of the Household Registration Act requires registration to be moved out once you have been out of Taiwan for two years or more. Taipei City's summary of the consequences lists loss of NHI eligibility from the move-out date. Because there is no minimum stay from 50, MM2H never forces you towards that two-year line. Coming home regularly keeps it well out of reach.

**National Health Insurance (健保).** Until 2024 many Taiwanese living part of the year abroad suspended their NHI. That option has gone. From 23 December 2024 the NHI Administration stopped accepting suspension applications, and the Ministry of Health and Welfare's position is that anyone with a household registration must stay insured and keep paying premiums, abroad or not. Treat NHI premiums as a fixed cost of your MM2H years, not a saving.

{% callout tone="info" title="NHI is not automatically your MM2H insurance" %}
MOTAC asks applicants aged 60 and below for health insurance with worldwide cover (p.23), and lists health insurance among the renewal documents without repeating the age qualifier (p.24). NHI reimburses emergency treatment abroad after the event, within its own limits. That is not the same as a worldwide policy, so get in writing what will be accepted before you rely on NHI alone.
{% endcallout %}

## What still needs you in Malaysia

No minimum stay does not mean MM2H can be run from Taipei. Some steps need you here in person, at any age.

1. **After approval.** The deposit is placed with a Malaysian bank, the medical is taken at a MOTAC panel clinic, and the pass is endorsed in your passport (p.23). Plan one trip for this, long enough to open the account and wait for results.
2. **The property.** Buying a residence is compulsory on every tier, and it may not be sold for ten years unless you trade up (p.20). The purchase must complete within one year of endorsement. ((MYPVIP practice, as at 3 August 2026))
3. **Renewal.** In practice the renewal is submitted with you in Malaysia, because it needs a copy of your latest Malaysian entry stamp. ((MYPVIP practice, as at July 2026)) Taiwan passport holders can use the automated gates at KLIA and KLIA2, and an e-gate entry does not produce a stamp, so raise this early in the year your renewal falls due.

The full list, including the dependant and medical rules, is in [MM2H requirements for applicants aged 50 and over](/insights/how-to/mm2h-over-50-requirements-2026/).

**Sarawak MM2H** is cheaper to enter, but its stay rule applies to the main applicant every year regardless of age. For a Taiwanese applicant whose problem is time, it solves the wrong problem. The two programmes are compared in full in [federal MM2H vs Sarawak MM2H](/insights/comparisons/federal-mm2h-vs-sarawak-mm2h/).

**Silver or Gold** suits most retirees. Silver is the smallest commitment, but the property must be held for ten years while the pass runs {{mm2h-silver:tenureYears:years}}, so it is a better fit if you already intend to renew. Gold's {{mm2h-gold:tenureYears:years}} covers the holding period with room to spare.

**Platinum** is the only tier that lets you work or run a business in Malaysia (p.20). If you still have a company in Taiwan and want a Malaysian operation alongside it, that is the reason to pay for it. If you do not, it buys rights you will not use. [MM2H Silver vs Gold vs Platinum](/insights/comparisons/mm2h-silver-vs-gold-vs-platinum/) works through the choice in more detail.

## Bringing your spouse or parents

A spouse, parents and parents-in-law can all be registered as dependants, with no age limit stated for parents (p.20). Each dependant adds fees rather than capital: a processing fee of {{mm2h-silver:processingFee.dependant:money}}, a pass fee of {{mm2h-silver:passFeePerYear.dependant:money}} for each year of the approved term, and a multiple-entry visa fee and security bond set by nationality. The [cost calculator](/tools/cost-calculator/) prices those last two for a Taiwan passport.

Register your spouse on the original application. On the death of the principal, the guide makes the pass transferable to the next of kin among the registered dependants, and a spouse already on the pass is in a far better position than one applying later.

## Tax in both directions

Holding MM2H does not make you a Malaysian tax resident, and spending only part of the year here usually keeps you outside Malaysia's 182-day test. How that works is covered in [is foreign income taxed in Malaysia](/insights/expat-living/is-foreign-income-taxed-in-malaysia/).

The Taiwan side matters more for most readers. While you remain a Taiwan tax resident, the Ministry of Finance's minimum tax rules bring overseas income into the basic income amount once a household's total for the year reaches NT$1 million. A part-year in Malaysia does not change that, and income you earn or receive in Malaysia can count towards it.

{% callout tone="warning" title="Verify current rules" %}
Tax residency, the minimum tax thresholds and NHI premium rules are set by Taiwan and change by regulation. Confirm your own position with a Taiwan tax adviser and the NHI Administration before you plan your year around them.
{% endcallout %}

## A worked example: a couple from Taichung

A principal aged 58 and a spouse aged 55. One still runs a small trading company in Taichung; the other looks after an elderly parent. They want to spend Chinese New Year and a month each autumn in Penang, about seven weeks a year in total, and keep their home, household registration and NHI in Taiwan.

Under 50, that pattern would fall short of the 90 days. At their ages there is no count to meet. They choose Gold, so that the pass outlasts the ten-year property hold, buy an apartment in Penang at the state's foreign-buyer floor rather than the national minimum, and register the spouse as a dependant. Neither works in Malaysia, so Platinum is not needed. Their NHI premiums continue as before, and because they are home most of the year, the two-year household registration line never comes into view.

The first year is the heavy one: a trip after approval for the deposit, medical and endorsement, and more for choosing and completing the property. After that, the pass asks nothing of their calendar beyond the renewal, and the two visits a year they wanted anyway are enough.
