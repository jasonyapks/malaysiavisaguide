# Malaysia Visa Guide — Branding Template

**Brand:** Malaysia Visa Guide  
**Domain:** malaysiavisaguide.com  
**Positioning:** The trusted independent guide for people exploring Malaysian visas, residency and long-term living.

## 1. Core Brand Idea

### Brand Promise
**Clarity for your journey to Malaysia.**

### Recommended Tagline
**Your Guide. Your Future. Malaysia.**

The brand should feel like a premium, authoritative guide to building a life in Malaysia — not a government department, generic travel agency, or generic visa consultancy.

## 2. Logo Concept

### The Guided Passport™

Combine three ideas into one distinctive symbol:

- Passport / document silhouette
- Compass / navigation needle
- Subtle Malaysian identity through a crescent/star reference

The icon should be geometric, simple, memorable, and forward-facing.

### Logo Personality

**Premium · Intelligent · Trustworthy · Directional · Malaysian**

### Avoid

- Literal Malaysian flag
- Cartoon passport imagery
- Airplane icons
- Generic globe symbols
- Checkmark-only logos
- Government-style crests
- Excessive gradients

### Primary Lockup

**[ICON] MALAYSIA**  
**   VISA GUIDE**

“Malaysia” carries authority while “Visa Guide” provides clarity.

## 3. Color System

| Color | Hex | Primary Use |
|---|---|---|
| Primary Navy | `#071D3A` | Foundation, headers, premium backgrounds |
| Royal Blue | `#124E9E` | Digital UI, secondary sections |
| Signature Gold | `#D4A017` | Accent, CTA emphasis, key information |
| Mist | `#E6E8EB` | Cards, dividers, secondary backgrounds |
| Off White | `#F7F7F9` | Main light background |

### Recommended Usage Ratio

- 60% Navy / Off-white
- 20% Royal Blue
- 10% Gold
- 10% Supporting neutrals

Gold is an accent, not the dominant color.

## 4. Typography

### Headlines
**Poppins SemiBold / Bold**

Use for:
- Hero headlines
- Section titles
- Campaign headlines
- Major statistics

### Body
**Inter Regular / Medium**

Use for:
- Website copy
- Articles
- Visa information
- Tables
- Forms
- Mobile UI

### Optional Editorial Typeface
**DM Serif Display**

Use sparingly for premium editorial statements or campaign headlines.

## 5. Brand Personality

| Trait | Expression |
|---|---|
| Trusted | Evidence, transparency, clear explanations |
| Professional | Precise, structured communication |
| Welcoming | Human and approachable |
| Knowledgeable | Educational rather than promotional |
| Aspirational | Focus on the life people want to build |
| Modern | Digital-first and visually clean |

### Voice

**Confident, clear, calm and helpful.**

Prefer:
> Understand your options. Choose the right pathway. Move forward with confidence.

Avoid aggressive sales language such as:
> GET YOUR MALAYSIA VISA NOW!!!

## 6. Iconography

Create a proprietary rounded-line icon system.

### Core Icons

- Passport
- Visa
- Application
- Approval
- Document
- Checklist
- Shield
- Consultation
- Family
- Education
- Business
- Property
- Retirement
- Malaysia
- Calendar
- Bank / Finance
- Support

### Icon Rules

- 1.75–2px stroke
- Rounded corners
- Minimal geometry
- No cartoon-style filled icons
- Navy as the primary color
- Gold only for emphasis
- Consistent 24px / 32px / 48px grids

## 7. Graphic Language

### Signature Device: The Compass Arc

Use a thin gold arc inspired by a compass trajectory.

Applications:
- Website section dividers
- Hero backgrounds
- Brochure corners
- Social media graphics
- Presentation covers
- Business cards
- Advertising
- Infographics

### Secondary Device: Directional Lines

Thin diagonal gold lines represent:

**Movement → Direction → Progress**

Keep them subtle.

## 8. Photography Direction

Photography should communicate **life in Malaysia**, not immigration paperwork.

### Preferred Subjects

- International families
- Professionals
- Entrepreneurs
- Retirees
- Students
- Couples

### Preferred Locations

- Kuala Lumpur skyline
- Modern residences
- Cafés
- Business districts
- International schools
- Beaches
- Malaysian lifestyle scenes

### Photography Treatment

**Natural photography + deep navy overlays + subtle gold accents**

Avoid:
- Stock-photo handshakes
- Excessive passport close-ups
- Visa stamps as the main visual
- Generic airport photography
- Overly staged corporate photography

The emotional message is:

> This is the life you're moving toward.

## 9. Website Design Language

The website should feel closer to a **premium financial/consulting platform** than a traditional immigration agency.

### Homepage Hero

> **Your Guide.**  
> **Your Future. Malaysia.**

Supporting line:

> Independent, clear and practical guidance on Malaysian visas, residency and long-term living.

Primary CTA:

**Explore Visa Options**

Secondary CTA:

**Talk to an Expert**

### Program Navigation

Use elegant cards for:

- **PVIP** — Premium Visa Programme
- **MM2H** — Malaysia My Second Home
- **Other Visa Options**

Each card receives its own icon and concise description.

## 10. Information Design

Malaysia Visa Guide should be known for making complicated immigration information incredibly easy to understand.

### Comparison Cards

Use structured comparison tables for:
- Age
- Duration
- Financial requirements
- Work rights
- Study rights
- Dependant options
- Other key conditions

Use gold for important numbers and decision points.

## 11. Social Media System

Every post should be identifiable even without seeing the logo.

### Content Categories

**01 — VISA GUIDE**  
Educational content

**02 — MALAYSIA LIFE**  
Lifestyle and relocation

**03 — VISA NEWS**  
Policy updates

**04 — EXPERT INSIGHT**  
Professional commentary

**05 — SUCCESS STORIES**  
Client journeys

### Recommended Social Format

Category:
> VISA GUIDE / PVIP

Headline:
> **CAN PVIP HOLDERS WORK IN MALAYSIA?**

Key answer:
> **YES — SUBJECT TO THE APPLICABLE CONDITIONS**

Use strong typography, navy backgrounds, white space, and restrained gold accents.

## 15. Brand Rules

### DO

- Use deep navy
- Use restrained gold
- Use generous whitespace
- Use strong typography
- Use clear grids
- Use editorial photography
- Use simple diagrams
- Use clear information hierarchy
- Maintain premium minimalism

### DON'T

- Overuse Malaysian flags
- Use too much gold
- Use cheap gradients
- Use clip-art passports
- Use generic globe imagery
- Use excessive shadows
- Crowd layouts
- Use red/green approval aesthetics
- Use aggressive sales language

The desired perception:

Not:

> Another visa agent.

**Authority + Guidance + Premium + Malaysia + Clarity**

### Visual Equation

+ **Gold opportunity**  
+ **Modern typography**  
+ **Compass symbolism**  
+ **Editorial information design**

= **Malaysia Visa Guide**

All future Malaysia Visa Guide branding, website design, social media graphics, advertisements, brochures, presentations, PDFs, and marketing materials should follow this template unless a deliberate campaign-specific exception is approved.

Premium, modern, authoritative, approachable, Malaysian, directional, information-led.

**Primary visual signature:**  
Navy + gold + Guided Passport + Compass Arc + clean editorial layouts.
