Fee Details

Enrolment Fees

The following fees are applicable upon application and enrolment.

Application Fee

RM1,300

A non-refundable fee, payable by credit/debit card at the end of the online application form upon submission.

Registration Fee

Early Years Full Day Programme: RM10,000 Upon entering Year 1, an additional Registration Fee of RM10,000 is applicable.

Early Years Half Day Programme: RM5,000 A top-up fee of RM5,000 (for entry into Reception) and RM10,000 (for entry into Year 1) is applicable.

Years 1 – 13: RM20,000

This is a non-refundable one time payment upon acceptance of the Letter of Offer issued by the School.

Deposit

An equivalent of one term’s tuition fee is required as deposit. The amount is to be topped up as tuition fees increases according to year level.

Terms & Conditions regarding Deposit

The deposit is refundable provided one term’s written notice of withdrawal is given in advance while the student is in school. The said notice must be received and acknowledged by the School on or prior to the commencement of the term the student will be withdrawn.

Visa and Residency Requirements

International learners must hold the appropriate visa to study in Malaysia. For learners attending day school without boarding facilities, they will be required to reside with their parent(s) or a legal guardian. Where a legal guardian is appointed, official court-issued guardianship documents are required to support eligibility.

For learners residing in the boarding house, a legal guardian must be formally appointed to provide consent and assume responsibility for the learner’s stay in Malaysia at the boarding house.

Certain visa holders and/or their dependents (e.g., Employment, Resident, Diplomatic, Malaysia My Second Home (MM2H), PVIP or Digital Nomad Pass holders) are required to obtain a yearly Permission to Study stamp before commencing their studies. Learners without these visas are required to apply for a Student Pass, which also allows for one Guardian Pass and dependent passes accompanying the Guardian Pass for younger non-school-going siblings under the age of 3.

The School supports families by preparing the necessary enrolment documents and applying for a Ministry of Education (MOE) Letter of Support to facilitate Immigration submission. As Immigration approval can take up to three months or more, particularly during peak periods, families are strongly encouraged to begin the process early and seek guidance from our admissions department.

Enrolment at Nexus is conditional upon the learner and their parent(s) or legal guardian, holding valid visas issued by the Malaysian Immigration Department. Learners may only commence school once the necessary approvals have been received.

Sales & Service Tax (SST)

Starting 1 July 2025, all private and international schools in Malaysia are required to charge 6% Service Tax (SST) on annual school fees exceeding RM60,000 per student. This applies to all school fees, including tuition fees, EAL charges, Application and Registration fees (for new students only), and this will affect all students regardless of nationality, except Malaysian students with OKU cards who are exempted.

Please note that all fees stated are exclusive of SST.

Tuition fees include curriculum day trips.

Cost of textbooks, uniform and overnight trips are excluded from the above Tuition fees. Learners from Year 5 onwards will need to buy an iPad or MacBook depending on the requirement for their year.

Annual Fee Reviews

All fees are reviewed annually and subject to change. Nexus Malaysia reserves the right to revise the terms and conditions on an annual basis.

Additional Support Fees

English as Additional Language (EAL) & Foundation English Program (FEP) fees are charged for those learners who need additional language support up to an Intensive English Programme, which will be assessed as part of the Admissions Process. Places are limited.

  • EAL Intensive English Programme or Foundation English Program (FEP) – 20% of tuition fee per term.

Alternative Pathway /Learning Support fees are charged for Learners with Special Education Needs (SEN) who need additional support. Places are based on a detailed assessment as part of the Admissions Process and are limited.

  • Learning Support: RM 5,000/term
  • Alternative Pathway: RM 16,000/term

Sundry Deposit: What is it?

Sundry Deposit is a sum of money which parents pay when they register their child at Nexus Boarding House. The deposit may be refunded provided:

  • The parent has given to the School ONE (1) full academic term’s notice. The said notice must be received and acknowledged by the School on or prior to the commencement of the term the student will be withdrawn.
  • There are no other outstanding monies to be paid (this includes but not limited to outstanding fees, damage to school property, etc);
  • All returnable items have been returned with nothing owed to the school

Boarding Fees - What’s Included

The Boarding Fees include accommodation, meals and laundry.

Advance Tuition Fee Rebate

Students will be given a 3% rebate on Tuition Fees for Terms 2 and 3 if the annual Tuition Fee is paid in full on or before the first day of Term 1.

Should a Learner leave Nexus Malaysia during or within an academic year and provided that adequate written notice of withdrawal is served according to the terms and conditions governing withdrawal, the prepaid Tuition Fee for the terms that the Learner will not be enrolled in will be refunded. However, the rebate granted at the beginning of academic year will be proportionately deducted from the prepaid Tuition Fee. For purpose of clarity, Tuition Fees paid in advance at the beginning of every term will not be pro-rated, even if a Learner ceases their studies in the middle of a term.

Siblings Discounts

Siblings are entitled to a discount on Upfront Fees and the Tuition Fee. It is only applicable to siblings who are still studying in the school. A sibling is defined as a legal brother or sister of a child enrolled in the school.

Families with siblings enrolled at the school are entitled to a 100% Application Fee rebate, a 50% (International)/100% (Malaysian) Registration Fee waiver, and a 5% Tuition Fee discount.

Payment Options

We accept fee payment by using either a local or international credit or debit card/account.

Simply choose the payment option that is most convenient for your family.

Online or Telegraphic Transfer

A bank transfer may be made to the School’s bank account.

For overseas transfers, please ensure that all transfer fees, both from the sending and receiving banks are fully covered by you, the sender. The full amount in Malaysian Ringgit must be received in the School’s bank account.

Kindly include your child’s name as a reference on the transaction and email the transaction slip to our admissions team.

Credit or Debit Card

New parents may pay for their child’s school registration fee, deposit and school fees using a credit card or debit card via at the Finance Counter at school or by requesting a Payment Link from our admissions team.

You may also pay for the annual school fees in advance at the beginning of the first term using a credit or debit card. By paying for a full year’s fees in advance, you can enjoy a 3% rebate on Term 2 and Term 3 tuition fees.

Flywire (Overseas payments)

Nexus Malaysia has partnered with Flywire to allow you to pay seamlessly using your overseas account or credit card. With Flywire you can:

  • Save on bank fees
  • Track your payments from start to finish
  • Contact their multi-lingual customer service team
