BNM MEDICAL INSURANCE:
- BNM press release 20 Dec 2024, "Interim measures to assist policyholders..." https://www.bnm.gov.my/-/mhit-pr-bm — premium adjustments from medical claims inflation spread over at least three years, in place until end-2026; policyholders 60+ on minimum plan get a one-year pause; age-band increases excluded.
- BNM Annual Report 2025, ch1c https://www.bnm.gov.my/publications/ar2025/ch1c — by June 2025 nearly 200,000 policyholders aged 60+ had premium hikes deferred; >15,000 reinstated without extra underwriting; "premium increase for over 90% of repriced policies due to medical inflation was below 10%"; base MHIT plan pilot 2H 2026, full rollout early 2027, DRG payment a core feature.
- BNM Budget 2027 statement, 9 Oct 2026 https://www.bnm.gov.my/-/belanjawan2027 — "MediAsas will be rolled out to the public in January 2027." Eligibility of non-citizens not stated in any source found.
